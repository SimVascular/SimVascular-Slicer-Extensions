What is ported, and what is not
-------------------------------

Ports of geometry3Sharp, and derivative works of it:

  svremesh/remesh.py        from `Remesher`, `RemesherPro`, `MeshConstraints`,
                            `MeshConstraintUtil`, `DCurveProjectionTarget` and
                            `MeshProjectionTarget`
  svremesh/dynamic_mesh.py  from `DMesh3`

They are ports rather than copies -- the language differs, the edge storage
differs, and several of the rules differ, each departure recorded in the
module's own docstring -- but a port is a derivative work, and both files are
distributed under the Boost licence terms below as well as the project's own.

Original to this project, containing no geometry3Sharp code:

  svremesh/__init__.py, svremesh/conditioning.py, svremesh/labelled.py,
  svremesh/quality.py, svremesh/surfaces.py, FaceAwareRemesh.py,
  tests/test_conditioning.py, tests/test_remesh.py

The tests name geometry3Sharp classes where they state which behaviour is being
held to the original's; they contain no ported code.
